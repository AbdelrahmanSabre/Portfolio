Case Study 01 · Pharaohs Development

When a real estate company says don't buy real estate.

In the loudest part of the real estate season, the category was asking people to buy. Pharaohs opened with the one sentence a real estate brand normally would not say: “ماتشتريش عقارات دلوقتي”. The wider Expo operation later reported 200M+ EGP in sales.

01 / STORY

The market was loud. So we chose to say the opposite.

The situation

At one of the strongest periods of the year for real estate, competitors were launching new projects and their exhibitions were already approaching. The market was full of reasons to buy.

Pharaohs chose a different entry point for its Expo. Before talking about six projects, payment plans or offers, the campaign needed one thing first: attention strong enough to make people ask what was going on.

01
The tension

Everyone was saying “buy.”

So repeating the normal real estate message would make Pharaohs sound like everyone else. The challenge was not simply to announce another exhibition. It was to make the market stop and look.

Crowded category → familiar message
02
The concept

Break the category rule.

Use a message that creates a contradiction: a real estate company telling people not to buy real estate. The line is simple, believable in tone and intentionally unfinished without the explanation that comes later.

Contradiction → curiosity
03
The trigger

“Wait. Why would they say that?”

The campaign was built to create that second thought. Instead of giving the audience the answer immediately, the first message created a gap that the next content had to close.

Curiosity gap → conversation
04
The reveal

Then we explained why.

The response video reframed the first statement and connected it to Pharaohs Expo: one place, multiple projects and a larger opportunity to compare and decide.

Conversation → context → Expo
THE HOOK

Say the one thing the category would never expect.

The first move

The campaign did not open with a project. It opened with a sentence that felt wrong coming from a real estate company.

The statement
“ماتشتريش عقارات دلوقتي.”
ExpectationReal estate brand → buy property.
ContradictionReal estate brand → don't buy property.
Question“Why would they tell me not to buy?”

It works as a campaign device because the audience is given the conflict before the explanation. The first post is not the complete story. It is the reason to continue watching.

02 / EXECUTION

One statement. Different faces.

How the statement became a conversation

The same message travelled through the people customers already associate with the company, each delivering it in their own natural way.

The chairman, the CEO and members of the sales team published videos from their own pages, each saying the same core line: “ماتشتريش عقارات”. The repetition made the message feel bigger than a single ad asset.

The result was not only reach. The message created discussion and confusion: why would a real estate company tell people not to buy real estate? That tension gave the campaign something most exhibition announcements struggle to create: a reason to wait for the answer.

03 / REVEAL

The answer had to make the first sentence worth it.

The response video

Once curiosity was high enough, the campaign switched from disruption to explanation.

The response video answered the question behind the statement: the point was not that property had suddenly become a bad idea. The point was that customers were being asked to slow down, compare opportunities and understand what Pharaohs Expo was bringing together in one place.

The important creative move is the sequence: contradiction → curiosity → conversation → explanation → Expo. The reveal only works because the first statement earned the audience's attention.

04 / OUTCOME

The campaign became the doorway to the Expo.

The “don't buy” statement was one part of a broader exhibition and sales operation. The campaign created the entrance: contradiction first, explanation second. The wider operation then carried the projects, offers, sales team and customer journey.

200M+ EGP reported in Expo sales across the broader operation

Attention was not the finish line.

The campaign opened the door. The wider Expo operation had to convert that attention through its project mix, offers, sales process and on-ground execution.
