An entrepreneurship experience that started with trying to build something of my own and turned into access to conversations and moments alongside Egypt's Prime Minister and Minister of Communications and Information Technology.

The story

The project did not continue. The experience did.

Building a startup teaches a different kind of business thinking. You have to shape an idea, explain it, defend the value, understand the people around it and deal with what happens when reality does not follow the plan.

What I took from it

You learn faster when the idea has to survive a real room.

From idea → presentation → conversation → reality.

The value of the experience was not simply who was in the room. It was what that environment forced me to do: make an idea clearer, think commercially, present it properly and understand how technology, business and public-sector priorities can meet.
